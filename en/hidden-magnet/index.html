Single-player puzzle · Three modes · Daily challenges

HOW TO PLAY

Out of sight.
Still a way through.

01 / Magnet

Hold, then guide gently.

Hold near the start to attract the ball, then move the magnet towards the goal. Sounds, edge flashes and vibration provide clues when the ball hits a wall.

02 / Echoes and notes

Listen for clues. Draw your route.

Use an echo to briefly reveal nearby walls, then record your discoveries with the drawing tool. Erase, undo or redo mistakes; your notes remain when you retry.

03 / Goal

Reach the hole. Reveal the answer.

Guide the entire ball into the gold-rimmed goal hole to reveal the maze, your route, completion time and star rating.

Tip: switching to the drawing tool releases the magnet, but the ball keeps moving. Pause first if you need time to think.

THREE WAYS TO EXPLORE

One magnet.
Three ways to explore.

Master magnetism, work against gravity,
then learn to change the paths themselves.

01

Flat

No gravity. Move the magnet across the plane.

The ball keeps its momentum when you release the magnet. Steer gently and use collision clues to discover hidden passages.

Easy / Normal / Hard · 30 levels each
02

Vertical

Use magnetism to support the ball against gravity.

The ball falls when you let go. Lift it with the magnet, navigate obstacles and choose the right moment to guide it into the hole.

Easy / Normal / Hard · 30 levels each
03

Dual-state maze

Trigger switches to alternate A/B paths.

Passing a diamond switch changes the doors: when A doors open, B doors close, and vice versa. Plan before you move.

No gravity · A separate 6-level chapter

A LITTLE CHALLENGE, EVERY DAY

A new maze every day.

Daily challenges rotate through difficulties and gravity modes. Record today’s route, find optional treasures and try to improve your best time and star rating.

Refreshes at midnight Hong Kong time. Daily challenges and campaign progress are recorded separately.

More to discover along the way.

Keys, doors and hidden treasures.

Some campaign mazes require a key to open a door, with optional treasures in side passages. You can finish without collecting them and return for an extra challenge.

Keep your route for next time.

Each level saves its own notes, fastest time and best star rating. Replaying never lowers an existing best result.
