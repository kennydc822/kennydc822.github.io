遊戲資料

隱形磁石是一款單人解謎遊戲，毋須註冊帳戶。關卡進度、最佳成績、畫面筆記及提示剩餘次數保存在裝置上；遊戲不會把這些內容上傳到我們的遊戲伺服器。本版本沒有自設多人遊戲伺服器或另行接入 Firebase Analytics。

廣告及私隱選擇

遊戲使用 Google AdMob 提供過關插頁廣告及由玩家主動選擇的獎勵廣告。完成獎勵廣告可補回相應提示次數；不觀看亦可繼續正常遊戲。

Google 廣告服務可能處理 IP 位址（用作估算大概位置）、裝置識別碼、廣告互動及診斷資料，以提供、衡量及維護廣告服務。實際處理視乎平台、權限及廣告設定。詳見 Google 私隱政策及Google 如何使用合作夥伴資料。

本版本不顯示 Apple ATT 追蹤授權要求。Google UMP 用於處理適用的廣告私隱選擇；如 SDK 要求提供更改選項，主畫面及暫停選單會顯示「廣告私隱設定」。沒有同意視窗並不代表廣告服務完全不處理資料。

兒童及混合受眾

遊戲面向包括兒童的混合受眾。本版本不收集出生日期，並統一使用 Google 兒童保護廣告請求、G 級廣告內容限制，以及 UMP 未達同意年齡設定。因此，本版本不向玩家要求個人化廣告同意。這些設定不等同於廣告服務完全不收集資料。

保留、刪除及聯絡

本機紀錄會保留至遊戲資料被清除；裝置或系統備份可能保留副本。移除 App 或清除其資料可能刪除進度，且不能由我們復原。Google 處理的資料依其政策保留，可能在其他國家處理。

如有私隱查詢，或家長對兒童資料有疑問，請聯絡 kennytsang3work@gmail.com。來信會包含你提供的電郵及內容，僅用於處理查詢；請勿傳送不必要的個人資料。視所在地適用規定，你可要求查閱、更正或刪除我們持有的資料；本機遊戲紀錄並非由我們遠端持有。

功能或資料處理方式改變時，我們會更新本頁及日期。此頁適用於隱形磁石，並非其他遊戲的私隱政策。

Privacy Policy

Hidden Magnet is a single-player puzzle game by Kenny Tsang. No account is required. Progress, best results, drawing notes and hint balances are stored on your device, not uploaded to our game server. This version has no multiplayer backend or separately integrated Firebase Analytics.

Advertising

Google AdMob supplies interstitial ads after completed levels and optional rewarded ads that replenish the selected hint. Watching a rewarded ad is not required for normal play.

Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and maintain advertising. Processing depends on platform, permissions and settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and partner data information.

This version does not request Apple ATT tracking permission. Google UMP manages applicable privacy choices. When required by the SDK, “Ad privacy settings” is available from the home and pause menus. The absence of a consent form does not mean advertising services process no data.

Children and mixed audiences

The game includes children in its audience. This version does not collect dates of birth and applies Google's child age treatment, a G ad-content limit and UMP under-age-of-consent treatment to all players. It therefore does not ask players for consent to personalized advertising. These controls do not mean no data is processed.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain until app data is removed; device or system backups may retain copies. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase progress, which we cannot restore. Google's retention and international processing are governed by its policies.

For privacy requests or parental concerns, contact kennytsang3work@gmail.com. Your email address and message are used to handle your enquiry; please avoid unnecessary personal information. Depending on applicable rules, you may request access, correction or deletion of information we hold. We do not remotely hold your local game records.

We will update this page and its date when functionality or data handling changes. This policy applies only to Hidden Magnet.
