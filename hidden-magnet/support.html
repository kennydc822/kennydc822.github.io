常見問題

點樣控制小球？

喺起點附近按住畫面吸引隱藏小球，再慢慢拖動磁石向終點探索。碰撞聲、畫面邊緣閃光及震動會提供線索。可以用回聲查看附近牆壁，再用畫筆記低路線。

有咩模式？

遊戲提供平面、傳送迷宮、正反迷宮及機關迷宮，合共 180 個固定關卡，另有每日挑戰。

回聲或足跡提示用完點算？

每關嘅回聲、鑰匙方向及足跡回放各有一次免費使用機會，使用紀錄會保存。用完後，可選擇完成獎勵廣告補回相應提示一次；唔睇廣告亦可以繼續正常遊戲。重試或重新開啟遊戲唔會重置提示次數。

廣告未就緒，或者睇完冇獎勵？

請檢查網絡連線，稍後再試。廣告供應會受網絡及供應情況影響；提早關閉或未成功完成廣告唔會獲得提示。如完成後仍未收到獎勵，請聯絡我們，並提供發生時間及提示種類。

需要帳戶或網絡先玩到嗎？

毋須註冊帳戶，主要單人玩法及每日迷宮可離線遊玩。廣告及獎勵廣告需要網絡連線。

進度同筆記保存喺邊？

關卡進度、最佳成績、筆記及提示剩餘次數保存在裝置上，冇遊戲帳戶雲端同步。刪除 App、清除資料或轉換裝置可能令進度遺失，我們無法從伺服器還原。遇到問題時，請先聯絡我們，避免以刪除重裝作為第一步。

每日挑戰幾時更新？

每日按香港時間（UTC+8）午夜更新，並使用裝置日期產生迷宮。請確認裝置日期及時間正確；完成目前一局後，返回每日挑戰頁查看新挑戰。

點樣改語言、聲音或震動？

到首頁「設定」調整語言、聲音及震動。遊玩期間亦可在暫停選單調整聲音及震動。

私隱問題？

請參閱私隱政策，或透過上述電郵聯絡我們。如廣告服務要求提供私隱選項，遊戲會顯示「廣告私隱設定」入口。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the ball?

Press near the start to attract the hidden ball, then slowly drag the magnet towards the goal. Collision sounds, edge flashes and vibration provide clues. Use Echo to briefly reveal nearby walls and draw notes to record your route.

Which modes are included?

Explore Flat, Teleport, Front & Back and Mechanism mazes, with 180 fixed levels in total, plus a daily challenge.

What if I run out of hints?

Echo, key-direction and trail replay each have one free use per level, saved independently. Once used, you can choose to complete a rewarded ad for one additional use of that hint. Hints are optional; you can keep playing without watching an ad. Retrying or restarting the game does not reset hint allowances.

Why is an ad unavailable, or a reward missing?

Check your internet connection and try again later. Ad availability depends on network conditions and supply. Closing an ad early or not successfully completing it does not grant a hint. If you completed an ad but did not receive the reward, contact us with the time and hint type.

Do I need an account or internet access?

No account is required. Core single-player gameplay and daily mazes work offline. Ads and rewarded ads require an internet connection.

Where are my progress and notes saved?

Progress, best results, notes and remaining hints are stored on your device. There is no game-account cloud sync. Deleting the app, clearing its data or changing devices may cause progress to be lost, and we cannot restore it from a server. Please contact us before trying a reinstall to fix an issue.

When does the daily challenge change?

It updates at midnight Hong Kong time (UTC+8), using your device's date. Check that your device date and time are correct. Finish your current attempt, then return to the daily challenge page to see the new challenge.

How do I change language, sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen to change language, sound and vibration. Sound and vibration can also be adjusted from the pause menu during play.

Privacy questions?

Read our Privacy Policy or contact us using the email above. If required by the advertising service, an “Ad privacy settings” entry will be available in the game.
